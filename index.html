100 years of Keeping NYC Comfortable

In 1927, a small oil burner company opened its doors on Long Island. A century later, Air Ideal keeps New York City buildings warm in winter and cool in summer, from pre-war walk-ups to glass towers. Here's how we got here, one decade at a time.

Follow our 100 years Contact Air Ideal

A Legacy Built by Generations

Air Ideal began as the Ideal Oil Burner Company, a family business that kept furnaces and boilers running for homes and shops across Nassau County. Three generations of that family grew it into one of the region’s most trusted commercial HVAC contractors.

When the family was ready to step back, they didn’t sell to an outside firm. They handed the company to three of their own people, employees who came up through the shop and knew every customer by name. Today they lead Air Ideal with the same promise made in 1927: answer the call, do the job right, and stand behind the work.

  • 1927The founding family opens the Ideal Oil Burner Company.
  • 1950The company adds cooling and becomes Air Ideal.
  • TodayOwned and led by three long-time employees.

100 Years Through the Decades

Every system we install starts with a single duct run. Follow ours from the coal-and-steam city of 1927 to the clean-energy buildings of today.

1927

to the 1930s

Where it began

The Ideal Oil Burner Company opens for business while the skyline races upward. The work is heat: oil burners, boilers and steam systems for homes, shops and apartment houses across Long Island and Queens. Air conditioning is still a novelty found in movie palaces.

1940s

to the 1960s

A changing industry

After the war, cooling moves from theaters into offices and homes. In 1950 the company adds air conditioning to its trade and takes a new name: Air Ideal. By the 1960s our crews are installing central air in new Midtown towers and Long Island office parks.

1970s

to the 1990s

The next generation

A new generation of the family takes over just as the energy crisis changes how buildings use power. Air Ideal starts offering preventive maintenance agreements, so owners spend less on emergencies and more on systems that last.

2000s

to the 2020s

Growing with the city

As New York builds up and out, Air Ideal grows with it. We open a Manhattan office, launch 24/7 emergency service, and take on design-build work for hospitals, schools, law firms, restaurants and luxury residences.

2020s

to 2027

The next chapter

Three long-time employees take ownership, keeping Air Ideal independent and local. Today’s work is cleaner air, lower energy use and smarter controls, helping buildings meet Local Law 97 and get ready for the next 100 years.
